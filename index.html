Our story

Fixed kitchen. Flexible brands.

One kitchen on Leigh Broadway. A rotating cast of food ideas. We create, test and develop every brand in the real world, with real feedback from the people who collect it.

Nothing is launched from a spreadsheet. A dish goes on the pass, you try it, you tell us what you think, and the menu moves. The brands that earn their place stay. The ones that don’t make room for the next idea.
